# Getting help with MeshLive

- **Using the app.** Start with the Android [user guide](https://github.com/MeshLive/meshlive-android/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md) and the list of [supported phones](https://github.com/MeshLive/meshlive-android/blob/main/docs/devices.md). The Windows and Apple (iPhone and Mac) repositories describe their own setup in their READMEs.
- **Something is broken.** Open an issue in the repository of the app you use, with the **Bug report** form: [Android](https://github.com/MeshLive/meshlive-android/issues/new/choose), [Windows](https://github.com/MeshLive/meshlive-windows/issues/new/choose), [iPhone and Mac](https://github.com/MeshLive/meshlive-apple/issues/new/choose).
- **A benchmark result looks odd.** Use the forms in [meshlive-benchmarks](https://github.com/MeshLive/meshlive-benchmarks/issues/new/choose).
- **Security or privacy problem.** Never in a public issue. See the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
- **Child safety concern.** See the [child safety standards](https://meshlive.github.io/child-safety/).
- **Anything else.** Write to [meshlive.dev@gmail.com](mailto:meshlive.dev@gmail.com).

MeshLive is a volunteer project, so answers are best effort. Please do not post photos, recordings or screenshots of other people, Bluetooth addresses or device serial numbers.
